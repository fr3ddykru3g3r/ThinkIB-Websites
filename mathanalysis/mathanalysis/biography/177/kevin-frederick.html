Kevin Frederick

Kevin Frederick (he/him) co-authored the Pearson IB Applications and Interpretations textbooks (SL and HL) published in 2019 and has delivered a range of workshops, from differentiation and vertical alignment to calculator techniques and IB internal assessments. Kevin taught secondary mathematics and computer programming at international schools from 2004 to 2021, leading mathematics departments at five schools on three continents. An elite endurance athlete and dedicated father to two Gen-Z children, Kevin is currently on sabbatical from the classroom in Bend, Oregon, USA and is returning to the classroom at the Cascades Academy in August 2022. Most recently, he destroyed his knee snowboarding.
