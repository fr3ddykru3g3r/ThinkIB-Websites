Italian expansion

Here are 25 multiple choice questions on the role of Mussolini and Italy in increasing international tension in the 1920s and 1930s.

What did Mussolini describe in 1922 as ‘the true test of nationhood and manhood’?


 

'War is to men what child bearing is to women'. This Fascist slogan reflects a central belief of fascist ideology, the necessity for struggle.

 

What did Mussolini believe was necessary in order for Italy to become a ‘great power’?


 

By securing an empire around the Mediterranean Italy could secure access to the Atlantic ocean. This revived the Roman notion of 'mare nostrum' ('our sea'). See, for example, the speech that Mussolini made on the 10 June 1940 announcing the declaration of war.

 

What change to the Paris Peace Treaties did Mussolini achieve in 1924?


 

Fiume was acquired by the Pact of Rome. A decisive foreign policy success for Mussolini at a time when he was consolidating his dictatorship.

 

What agreement did Mussolini sign in 1928?


 

The international pact, brokered by the foreign sectretaries of the USA and France, was ultimately signed by 65 countries. It was an apparently meaningless piece of paper, certainly to Mussolini, but the signatories were renouncing the use of war as a legitimate political strategy, for the first time in human history. Mussolini saw the propaganda value of signing.

 

When Hitler came to power in 1933, what did Fascist and Nazi ideologies NOT have in common?


 

Mussolini and Hitler were not natural allies. Mussolini once called Hitler a 'racial lunatic'. This is important because it suggests that the relationship between Hitler and Mussolini, which developed in the 1930s and was so dangerous to international peace, was not inevitable.

 

How did Mussolini respond to the murder of Dollfuss in Austria in 1934?


 

This decisive action forced Hitler to back down from his intended Anschluss. Mussolini was following traditional Italian foreign policy which was to avoid having a strong power on the northern frontier.

 

What agreement did Mussolini make with Britain and France in 1935?


 

This agreement was a commitment to resist German alterations to the Treaty of Versailles. It seemed that Italy was an ally of Britain and France. Crucially at the conference, Britain and France made no mention of developments in...(see next question)

 

Where is Wal Wal?


 

A military confrontation at this obscure oasis in the desert in December 1934 led to the death of 150 Abyssinians and 50 Italian troops. It brought war between Italy and Abyssinia closer.

 

In which year had the Italian forces been defeated by Abyssinia at Adowa?


 

This historic defeat at Adowa had been a national humiliation and weakened the liberal regime which came before fascism.

 

How many men were used in the invasion of Abyssinia?


 

Mussolini used overwhelming force supported by air power and motorised technology. Mustard gas was also used against Abyssinians defending themselves with spears.

 

Who was declared Emperor of Italian Territories in East Africa’?


 

Mussolini drew as much propaganda value as he could from this conquest. He saw it as a vindication of fascist ideology and it strengthened further his belief in the use of aggression.

 

How much was the budget deficit in Italy after the Abyssinian invasion?


 

This was a large expense to the country, and there were ongoing costs of the occupation. Abyssinians waged a guerilla war until 1941 and 130 000 Italian troops had to be maintained in the country.

 

How did Italian women show their support for this invasion?


 

This was perhaps the high point of Mussolini's popularity.

 

What was the response of the League of Nations to the Abyssinian invasion?


 

The Suez Canal was not closed, and the sanctions did not include oil, which was essential for Mussolini's army. Britain and France were in a dilemma. They recognised the need to take action and show support for the League of Nations, but they did not want to push Mussolini into a closer relationship with Hitler. The effect of these actions was to fatally weaken the League of Nations AND bring Hitler and Mussolini closer in an alliance. 

 

Who did Mussolini support in the Spanish Civil War?


 

The Nationalists led by General France were challenged by the supporters of the Spanish Republic. The civil war was to last for three years. The Republicans received aid from the Soviet Union.

 

How many Italian troops were sent to Spain?


 

Mussolini was determined not to be outdone by Hitler, who was also supporting Franco. Officially the Italian troops were 'volunteers' but in reality many were from the regular Italian army.

 

What was the Italian debt in this war?


 

Mussolini's commitment to a 'Fascist victory'in Spain left a heavy cost and left Italy unprepared for war in 1939. Mussolini said that the Spanish civil War 'bled Italy white'.

 

What secret understanding was reached in the ‘Rome-Berlin Axis’ of 1936?

This agreement showed that Italy and Germany had been reconciled after the disagreement over the Anschluss in 1934. Hitler suggested that he was planning to be ready for war in three years. Mussolini was excited about the talk of war, although he was not committed to Italy being involved.

 

What did Hitler do in March 1938 without telling Mussolini?


 

Mussolini was inwardly furious but outwardly told Hitler that Italy would not intervene. He realised that there was nothing he could do to stop this, even though it was a significant setback for Italy and revered the policy he had pursued since 1930.

 

Which of the following was NOT alleged to be an imitation of Hitler by Mussolini?


 

In the later 1930s Mussolini became more unpopular in Italy. The Pope said that he was 'following the Germans' with the racial laws of 1938.

 

Who asked Mussolini to be the mediator at the Munich Conference?


 

It is significant that Chamberlain of Britain still regarded Mussolini as a mediator and potential peace-maker. Mussolini enjoyed the propaganda that came from this. However, he secretly colluded with Hitler at the conference to favour the Germans.

 

Which country did Italy invade in April 1939?


 

This was a desperate attempt to get back in the limelight after the German invasion of Czechoslovakia. Albania had been effectively under Italian control for the last ten years. There was no major fighting. It was commented that a 'well-organised fire brigade' could have resisted the Italian invasion.

 

What was the name first proposed by Mussolini for the ‘Pact of Steel’?


 

Mussolini was more obsessed with how things sounded than the content of the treaty! In fact he negotiated without the benefit of an interpreter as he claimed he could speak German. Mussolini was largely unaware of what he had signed up to but the final title 'Pact of Steel' pleased him very much.

 

How did Mussolini get out of the Pact of Steel agreement when war began in September 1939?

The Italians made such a long list of 'essential supplies' that it would have taken 17 000 train loads of equipment from Germany to meet their demands.

 

When did Italy intervene in World War Two?


 

The fall of France was imminent. Mussolini calculated that he could secure gains at the peace table if Italy had played some part in the war, which he thought was in its final stages. Ciano, the Italian foreign secretary wrote in his diary that there was little enthusiasm for the war. Ciano had always been against the German alliance.
