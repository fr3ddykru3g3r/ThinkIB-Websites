Studied English at Trinity Hall, Cambridge, taking Honours in 1971.

Left St Clare’s in 2002 and moved to Catalonia with his wife, where, in addition to maintaining his interest in the IB, and educational consultancy work, he is working towards the next exhibition of his paintings, deepening his knowledge of Spanish, and enjoying Spain.
