Mark Johnson

Mark Johnson began teaching in 1994, after graduating from the Institute of Education, London University.  After starting his career lecturing BTEC Business Management, he began teaching IB Economics and Business Management for the first time, in 1997, at United World College of SE Asia.  Throughout his 28 year teaching career Mark has also held the position of IB Coordinator and is an experienced workshop leader and educational consultant with the IBO, helping candidate schools gain accreditation to the programme.  Mark is a senior examiner for the IB, specialising in the IA process and the paper two examination.
