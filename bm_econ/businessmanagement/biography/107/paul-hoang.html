Paul Hoang

Paul Hoang has taught IB DP and IGCSE Business Management and Economics for over 25 years. He has held the positions of Vice Principal and IB Diploma Programme Coordinator at an internationally renowned IB World School in Hong Kong, and was previously Head of Department at two schools in London. He is an examiner for multiple examination boards.

Paul is the author of several best-selling publications including Economics for the IB Diploma (Hodder Education, and endorsed by the IB, Managebac, and Pamoja Education), IB Business Management 5th edn (IBID Press), IGCSE Business Studies for Edexcel (Anforme), and Cambridge IGCSE and O Level Economics (Hodder Education). He is also the lead author of Extended Essay for the IB Diploma: Skills for Success, published by Hodder Education.

Paul is an experienced IB Workshop Leader, with over 15 years experience of leading workshops in Business Management, Economics, TOK, and the Extended Essay in Europe, Asia Pacific, and North America.

Paul is a partner consultant for IB School Improvement, working with school leaders with a focus on the use of assessment data to enhance teaching and learning. He is also the Subject Leader for DP Business Management and DP Economics at Faria Education Group.
